Who it is for

If AI has moved on without you, that is a teaching problem — not a you problem.

Mid-career professionals

You are good at the job. The tools around it changed. Colleagues who “just get AI” can look quicker, and the fear of being left behind is rational. These sessions close that gap in your actual work, not in the abstract.

Small businesses

You do not need a 40-hour course. You need someone to sit with the tasks you already repeat — email, spreadsheets, documents, research — and show you a safer, faster way to use AI on them.

Afterwards

What you should be able to do after three sessions

  • Explain what these tools are for, and pick the right one for a given task.
  • Write prompts that get usable answers the first or second time, not the tenth.
  • Use Copilot or Gemini inside Excel, Outlook, Word and PowerPoint on work that looks like yours.
  • Save the setups you would otherwise rebuild every week, and follow a 30-day practice plan.
  • Keep employer-confidential data out of AI tools, on purpose, as a habit.
